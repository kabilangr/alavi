Alavi Privacy Policy

App: Alavi – Data usage meter & firewall (com.nikared.alavi)
Developer: NikaRed · Effective date: 25 November 2026

In short: Alavi does not collect, share or sell any data. Everything it reads stays on your phone. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere, not even to us. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.

What Alavi reads on your phone, and why

Alavi only reads information it needs to show your data usage and to block the apps you choose. You can decline any permission. The features that need it then stop working, and the rest of the app keeps working.

InformationWhy Alavi uses it
Per-app network usage (Usage access) To show how much mobile data and Wi-Fi each app uses, to show your history, and to check data plans and per-app limits.
Which app is on screen (Usage access) Only if you use "Only block in the background": the firewall needs to know whether that app is open. Alavi does not keep a record of which apps you open.
List of installed apps To show each app's name and icon next to its usage, and to let you choose apps to block or schedule.
SIM card details (slot, name, carrier) To show mobile data for each SIM and to apply rules and data plans to a chosen SIM.
Network state To know whether you're on Wi-Fi or mobile data, so the right block rules apply.
Battery level and charging state For the battery screen, which shows battery use and health on this phone.

The firewall (VPN service)

Alavi's firewall uses Android's VpnService to create a local VPN on your phone. It is not a VPN to a server: traffic never goes to Alavi or anyone else.

  • Only the apps you choose to block (by rule, data limit or schedule) are routed into the local VPN, and their traffic is dropped there.
  • Alavi does not read, inspect, log, change or redirect the contents of any traffic.
  • Apps you don't block are not routed through the VPN at all.
  • The firewall runs only when you turn it on. Android asks for your consent first, and you can turn it off at any time from the app, the Quick Settings tile or Android's VPN settings.

Where your data is stored

  • Usage history, block rules, limits, schedules, data plans and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Usage history is kept for up to about two years so you can compare billing cycles. Older entries are deleted automatically.
  • Android backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Alavi, so this data is not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone.
  • To delete everything, clear Alavi's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your phone. They show that the firewall is running, warn you about your data plan, tell you when an app reaches its data limit, and point out unusually high usage. You can turn them off in Alavi's settings or in Android Settings.

Sharing with third parties

Alavi shares no data with anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and it has no servers. Google Play may collect information about your download and installation under Google's privacy policy. Alavi has no access to that information.

Children

Alavi is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of Alavi ever needed to handle data differently, this policy would be updated before that version is released.
